Privacy Policy for Tidal

Last updated: August 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tidal ("Tidal," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). We built Tidal to help you track your menstrual cycle, and we know that means handling some of the most sensitive information there is. This policy is written to be read, not skimmed past — please take a few minutes with it.

Before you publish this: This document is a solid starting draft based on exactly what Tidal's code actually does today, but it is not legal advice, and we are not a law firm. Menstrual and reproductive health data is subject to extra-strict rules in a growing number of places (see "Sensitive Health Data" below) — have a lawyer familiar with health-data and privacy law in the regions where you'll operate review this before it goes live, especially if you'll have users in the EU/UK (GDPR), California (CCPA/CPRA), or Washington State (My Health My Data Act).

1. Who we are

[AJ / ANSH], the developer of Tidal, is the "data controller" responsible for the information described in this policy. If you have questions, see the "Contact Us" section below.

2. Information we collect

We designed Tidal to collect the minimum needed to make the app work. Specifically:

Account information

  • Email address and password, used to create and secure your account (handled by our authentication provider, Supabase — see "Third-party services" below). We never see or store your password in plain text; it is hashed by Supabase before it ever reaches our database.

Health and cycle data you enter

  • Period start/end dates and flow intensity
  • Symptoms you log (e.g. cramps, headache, bloating)
  • Mood entries
  • Free-text notes you write
  • Your cycle-length and period-length preferences, and your notification preferences

This is the core purpose of the app, and it is the most sensitive category of information we handle. We treat it accordingly (see Section 5).

Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect your precise location.
  • We do not access your contacts, camera, microphone, or photos.
  • We do not use advertising or analytics SDKs, and there are no ad networks integrated into the app.
  • We do not use cookies or cross-app/cross-site tracking of any kind.

3. How we use your information

We use the information above only to:

  • Create and maintain your account
  • Predict your next period and fertile window based on your own logged history
  • Show you your data in the Calendar, Today, and Insights screens
  • Send you the optional local reminder notifications you've turned on in Settings (these are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device — see Section 4)
  • Respond to you if you contact us for support

We do not use your health data for advertising, and we do not build advertising profiles from it.

4. Notifications

Tidal's reminder notifications (period coming up, log-your-period nudge, fertile window) are scheduled locally on your device using your device's own operating system. No cycle data is sent to a remote push-notification server to make these work, and no third party is involved in delivering them.

5. Data storage and security

Your data is stored in a managed Postgres database operated by Supabase, our backend provider. Specifically:

  • Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) between the app and our database.
  • Access to your data is enforced at the database level using Row-Level Security policies, meaning the database itself refuses any request for your data that isn't authenticated as you — this isn't just an app-level check.
  • We do not have a separate copy of your data anywhere else; Supabase is our sole data store.

No system is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security, but we've designed the app so that even we, as the developer, cannot casually browse user health data — access requires deliberately bypassing the same protections your own requests go through.

7. Your rights and choices

You are in control of your data at all times:

  • Access & export: Go to Settings → "Export my data" to download a complete copy of everything you've logged, in a single file, at any time.
  • Correction: Every entry you log can be edited or deleted directly in the app (Log tab).
  • Deletion: Go to Settings → "Delete my account" to permanently delete your account and every piece of data associated with it. This action is immediate and irreversible — we do not retain a hidden copy after you delete your account.
  • Withdraw consent for notifications: Turn off any or all reminder types in Settings at any time.

If you are located in the EU/UK, California, or another jurisdiction with statutory data rights (right to access, rectification, erasure, portability, restriction of processing, or to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority), those rights apply to you in addition to the in-app controls above. Contact us using the details in Section 12 to exercise any right that isn't already self-service in the app.

8. Data retention

We retain your data for as long as your account remains active, so the app can function (predictions need your history). If you delete your account, all associated data is permanently deleted from our systems immediately — we do not keep backups of deleted accounts beyond what's needed for standard, time-limited database backup rotation, after which it is gone entirely.

9. Sensitive health data — additional notice

Menstrual and reproductive health data receives special legal protection in a number of places, separate from general health-privacy law:

  • In the United States, several states (including Washington's My Health My Data Act and similar laws elsewhere) specifically regulate "consumer health data," which includes reproductive and menstrual health information, and give users rights beyond what applies to ordinary personal data.
  • If you are in the EU/UK, this data is very likely classified as a "special category" of personal data under GDPR/UK GDPR, requiring an explicit legal basis (in Tidal's case, your explicit consent when you choose to log this information).

We do not sell this data, and we do not share it with data brokers, advertisers, or law enforcement except where we are legally compelled to by a valid court order — and even then, only to the extent legally required, and we will attempt to notify you unless legally prohibited from doing so.

10. Children's privacy

Tidal is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13 (or under 16 in jurisdictions where that is the relevant age threshold, such as the UK). If we learn that we've inadvertently collected data from a child under the applicable age, we will delete it promptly. If you believe a child has used Tidal to create an account, please contact us.
